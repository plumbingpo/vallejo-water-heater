How to Tell Something Is Wrong

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you money and keep a slow drip from turning into a flooded utility room. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection
  • Fixtures that run too hot one minute and cool the next
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life
  • The tank giving up in the middle of a rush
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • A recirculation loop that no longer seems to do its job
  • A commercial heater that has grown noticeably louder

A Slow Leak Never Stays Slow

Simple Ways to Extend Your Water Heater's Life

A few preventive steps can help you avoid a cold shower or a wet floor down the road. Here are our recommendations:

  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Have mixing valves checked so fixtures stay neither scalding nor lukewarm
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Confirm the recirculation loop still delivers hot water quickly to the far fixtures
  • Check anodes on a schedule matched to your volume and water quality
  • Track temperatures at the dish machine and hand sinks as part of routine checks
