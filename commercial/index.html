Safe Installation

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A commercial water heater failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Vallejo and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done after hours to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back fast, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we put maintenance in place so it doesn't happen again, and every response is fully documented for your records.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Vallejo Water Heater program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Fewer breakdowns, longer-lasting tanks, and lower energy costs: that's what regular maintenance is designed to produce. All for a fraction of what an emergency replacement costs. Contact Vallejo Water Heater to build a plan around your property's needs and budget.
